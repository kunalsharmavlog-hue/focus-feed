# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-29)

First public release.

- **Topic Reels:** plays reels from Instagram's keyword search for your topics, one after
  another; skips reels you've seen and rotates through topics.
- **Feed & Reels filter:** keyword, always-hide and always-show-account rules, with presets
  for editing, coding, fitness and cooking. Non-matching reels are skipped automatically.
- **Learns your taste** from likes, saves, watch time and quick skips; favourite hashtags join
  Topic Reels.
- **Optional downloader:** "Download reel" button and save-on-like via a local yt-dlp helper,
  with H.264 conversion for editing apps and an opt-in login mode for reels that need it.
